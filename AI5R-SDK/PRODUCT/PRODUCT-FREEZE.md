# AI5R Product Layer Freeze

Status:
FROZEN

---

## Purpose

The Product Layer defines how AI5R platform products are described, built, registered, and executed.

AI5R Platform may produce multiple products, including:

- AI Factory
- AI Workforce
- AI Operating System
- AI Marketplace
- AI Studio
- AI Cloud
- AI Enterprise

---

## Core Components

- ProductObject
- ProductEngine
- ProductManifest
- ProductCatalog
- ProductRuntime

---

## Product Principle

AI5R Platform is NOT a single product.

AI5R Platform is a foundation for multiple products.

Each AI5R product SHALL have:

- Product Object
- Product Manifest
- Runtime
- Catalog Registration
- Tests
- Release Documentation

---

## Flagship Products

AI Workforce is one flagship product.

Digital Employee is a product artifact inside AI Workforce.

Digital Employee is NOT the whole AI5R platform.

---

## Compatibility Rule

Future product modules must preserve backward compatibility with this layer.
