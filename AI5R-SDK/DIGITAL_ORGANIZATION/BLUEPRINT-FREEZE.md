## Purpose

Organization Blueprint Runtime defines how reusable Digital Organization templates are registered and deployed into working Digital Organizations.

---

## Core Components

- OrganizationBlueprint
- BlueprintRuntime

---

## Blueprint Principle

An Organization Blueprint is a deployable template for a Digital Organization.

Blueprints may define:

- Industry
- Departments
- Positions
- Capabilities
- Workflows

---

## Current Capabilities

The runtime can:

- create Organization Blueprints
- register Blueprints
- deploy Blueprints into Organization Runtime
- generate departments from Blueprint definition
