# AI5R Business Layer Freeze

Status:
FROZEN

---

## Purpose

The Business Layer defines how AI5R products are commercialized, priced, delivered, supported, and cataloged.

AI5R is not only a technical platform.

AI5R is a business platform for producing and operating AI products, AI workforce, and managed digital organizations.

---

## Core Components

- BusinessModelObject
- BusinessEngine
- PricingModel
- PricingEngine
- CustomerSegment
- DeliveryModel
- BusinessCatalog
- BusinessRuntime

---

## Commercial Principle

Every AI5R product SHALL define:

- Product Code
- Target Customer
- Revenue Model
- Delivery Model
- Support Model
- Pricing Strategy
- Customer Segment
- Delivery Contract

## Flagship Business Product

AI Workforce is one flagship product.

Digital Employees may be offered as:

- Part-time AI workers
- Full-time AI workers
- Dedicated AI workers
- Managed by AI5R service

No AI5R product should be treated as complete until it has both:

1. Technical contract
2. Business contract
