# AX-303
# Cross-Domain Intelligence Framework

Version: 1.0

==================================================

VISION

Enterprise problems rarely belong
to only one department.

AI5R shall reason across domains
before presenting major conclusions.

==================================================

PURPOSE

Connect signals from multiple departments
to identify

- Shared causes
- Hidden dependencies
- Systemic risks
- Enterprise opportunities
- Cross-functional actions

==================================================

EVIDENCE CORRELATION

- Missing evidence

- Object relationship

==================================================

SYSTEMIC ROOT CAUSE

AI5R shall distinguish

Immediate Cause

The direct event before failure.

Contributing Factors

Conditions that increased likelihood.

Systemic Cause

Organizational weakness that allowed
the issue to continue or repeat.

==================================================

RECOMMENDATION STRUCTURE

Every cross-domain recommendation shall contain

- Executive Summary
- Primary Domain
- Related Domains
- Evidence
- Root Cause
- Business Impact
- Risk Level
- Recommended Actions
- Responsible Owners
- Priority
- Target Date
- Expected Health Improvement
- Confidence

==================================================

COORDINATED ACTION PLAN

Actions may be assigned across departments.

Example

Engineering

Inspect seal system.

Procurement

Review vendor and lead time.

Inventory

Set critical spare minimum stock.

HR

Validate technician competency.

Knowledge

Publish lessons learned.

Finance

Review cost impact.

Project

Update maintenance planning.

==================================================

HEALTH INTEGRATION

Cross-domain reasoning shall update

- Domain Health
- Related Domain Health
- Overall Enterprise Health
- Risk Trend
- Recommendation Status

==================================================

ESCALATION RULE

Escalate to enterprise leadership when

- More than two domains are materially affected
- Safety or legal exposure exists
- Financial impact exceeds threshold
- Critical asset availability is threatened
- Customer impact is high
- The issue is recurring
- Confidence is low but consequence is high

==================================================

HUMAN AUTHORITY

Cross-domain recommendations
support management decisions.

High-impact actions require
authorized human approval.

==================================================

AI5R PRINCIPLE

Do not optimize one department
while damaging the enterprise.

Think in systems.

Act across domains.
