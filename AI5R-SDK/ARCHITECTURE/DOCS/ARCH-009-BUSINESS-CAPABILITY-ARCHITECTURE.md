# ARCH-009 — Business Capability Architecture

## 1. Purpose

AI5R Enterprise OS is capability-based.

A company is not built from tables or isolated modules.

A company is built from business capabilities.

## 2. Core Principle

Business Capability is the bridge between:

- Enterprise Strategy
- Enterprise Operating Stack
- Business Documents
- Workflows
- Transactions
- Accounting
- Executive Intelligence

## 3. Enterprise Operating Flow

EnterpriseObject
↓
EnterpriseEvent
↓
BusinessCapability
↓
EnterpriseDocument
↓
EnterpriseWorkflow
↓
EnterpriseTransaction
↓
EnterpriseAccounting
↓
ExecutiveIntelligence

## 4. Business Capability Definition

A Business Capability is a reusable enterprise ability.

Examples:

- SELL
- BUY
- PAY
- RECEIVE
- SHIP
- STORE
- MANUFACTURE
- MARKET
- RECRUIT
- TEACH
- ASSESS
- SERVE
- ANALYZE

## 5. Locked Rule

No vertical module may bypass Business Capability.

The AI Organization Designer must not start from database tables.

It must start from business capabilities.
