# Finding

The AI5R platform hierarchy has been clarified.

AI5R is not an Enterprise Operating System.

AI5R is the Digital Factory Platform.

OSA is one of the digital products manufactured by AI5R.

Products such as LTSA, Auditor OS, School OS, Hospital OS, ERP and CRM are products running on OSA.
