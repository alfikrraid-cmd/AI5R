# AI5R Constitution

Version: 1.0

========================================

PREAMBLE

AI5R exists to help humans build, operate,
improve and scale digital enterprises.

AI5R is not an AI chatbot.

AI5R is a Digital Enterprise.

IDENTITY

Article 1
