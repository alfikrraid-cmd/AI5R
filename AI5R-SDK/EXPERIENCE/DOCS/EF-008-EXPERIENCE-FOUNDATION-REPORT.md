## Experience Responsibility

Experience converts Warehouse data into accountable observations.

Examples:

- A transcript becomes a technical observation
- A customer complaint becomes a service finding
- A field report becomes an operational observation
- A sensor reading becomes an anomaly observation
- A document becomes extracted evidence

## Architectural Boundaries

Experience is not Warehouse.

Warehouse records source material.

Experience observes and interprets source material.

Experience is not Memory.

Experience captures current observation.

Memory decides what should be retained across time.

Experience is not Knowledge.

Experience may contain uncertain observations.

Knowledge contains validated and reusable understanding.

## Core Principle

No Memory or Knowledge object should be created directly from Warehouse without passing through Experience.
