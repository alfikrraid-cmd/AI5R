# 06 Factory System

AI5R is an AI Manufacturing Platform.

Every AI5R product SHALL be manufactured through canonical manufacturing stations.

Canonical Manufacturing Line

Reality
↓
Warehouse
↓
Experience
↓
Memory
↓
Knowledge
↓
Innovation
↓
Capability
↓
Decision
↓
Execution

Every manufacturing station SHALL define:

- Input
- Validation
- Processing
- Output
- Quality Check
- Tests

Every manufactured object SHALL be reproducible.
