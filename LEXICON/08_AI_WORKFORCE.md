# AI Workforce

Status:
Stable

Version:
1.0

## Definition

AI Workforce is an AI5R product family that provides Digital Employees as part-time, full-time, dedicated, or managed digital workers.

## Purpose

To help customers access digital labor without building their own AI organization from scratch.

## Delivery Models

- Part-time Digital Employee
- Full-time Digital Employee
- Dedicated Digital Employee
- Managed by AI5R

## Examples

- CEO AI
- Marketing AI
- Finance AI
- Customer Service AI

## Related Terms

- Digital Employee
- Digital Organization
- Managed AI Workforce
