# Digital Employee

Status:
Stable

Version:
1.0

## Definition

A Digital Employee is an AI entity that operates as a worker within a Digital Organization.

Unlike a generic AI agent, a Digital Employee possesses organizational identity, assigned responsibilities, governance constraints, and defined capabilities.

## Purpose

To perform organizational work as a member of a department or team.
