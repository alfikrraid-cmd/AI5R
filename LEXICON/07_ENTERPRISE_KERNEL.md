# Enterprise Kernel

Status:
Stable

Version:
1.0

## Definition

The Enterprise Kernel is the internal orchestration layer that connects identity, organization, position, knowledge, capability, decision, execution, and response.

## Purpose

To ensure enterprise workflows are governed and coordinated.

## Rule

Execution must not bypass Decision.

Decision must not bypass Identity.
