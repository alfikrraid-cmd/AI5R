# Digital Organization

Status:
Draft

Version:

## Definition

A Digital Organization is a structured collection of Digital Employees operating under a shared organizational identity, governance model, mission, and workflow.

It represents an organization rather than a collection of unrelated AI agents.

## Purpose

To enable organizations to build AI-powered operational structures.
