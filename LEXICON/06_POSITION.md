# Position

Status:
Stable

Version:
1.0

## Definition

A Position is an organizational job title with authority, responsibility, reporting line, and approval scope.

A Position may contain multiple Cognitive Functions.

## Purpose

To define where a Digital Employee belongs in an organization.

## Examples

- CEO
- CFO
- Marketing Manager
- Finance Staff
- Customer Service Officer

## Related Terms

- Digital Employee
- Cognitive Function
- Organization
