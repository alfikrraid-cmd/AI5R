# Managed AI Workforce

Status:
Draft

Version:
1.0

## Definition

Managed AI Workforce is a service model where AI5R provides and operates Digital Employees on behalf of the customer.

## Purpose

To allow customers to benefit from Digital Employees without managing the technical or operational complexity.

## Examples

- Managed Marketing Team AI
- Managed Finance AI
- Managed Customer Service AI

## Related Terms

- AI Workforce
- Digital Employee
- Business Model
