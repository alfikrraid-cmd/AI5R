# Organization Blueprint

Status:
Draft

Version:
1.0

## Definition

An Organization Blueprint is a reusable specification describing the structure, roles, capabilities, workflows, and governance of a Digital Organization.

Blueprints allow complete organizations to be deployed consistently.

## Purpose

To accelerate creation of Digital Organizations.

## Examples

- Restaurant Blueprint
- School Blueprint
- Hospital Blueprint
- Consulting Blueprint
- Retail Blueprint

## Related Terms

- Digital Organization
- Digital Employee
- AI Workforce
