# Capability

Status:
Stable

Version:
1.0

## Definition

A Capability is the smallest operational ability that AI5R can use to perform work.

Capabilities are reusable units of action, analysis, generation, validation, or execution.

## Purpose

To define what a Digital Employee can do.

## Examples

- Analyze Risk
- Generate Marketing Plan
- Calculate ROI
- Search Knowledge
- Create Presentation
- Execute Workflow

## Related Terms

- Digital Employee
- Cognitive Function
- Execution
