## Definition

Blueprint Marketplace is an AI5R product where users can discover, install, sell, or customize Organization Blueprints, Capability Packs, Knowledge Packs, and Digital Employee Packs.

## Purpose

To create an ecosystem around reusable Digital Organization assets.
