# AI5R Research Map

Status:
Draft

Version:
1.0

## Purpose

The AI5R Research Map defines the intellectual foundation of AI5R.

AI5R is built from the synthesis of:

- Organization Science
- Knowledge Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Software Architecture
- Systems Engineering
- Governance
- Decision Science
- Business Model Innovation

## Research Rule

Every major AI5R theory, architecture, standard, and product SHALL be traceable to:

Reality
↓
Observation
↓
Question
↓
Research
↓
Hypothesis
↓
Theory
↓
Validation
↓
Knowledge
↓
Architecture
↓
Implementation
↓
Product

## Core Principle

Code follows Architecture.

Architecture follows Theory.

Theory follows Research.

Research follows Reality.
