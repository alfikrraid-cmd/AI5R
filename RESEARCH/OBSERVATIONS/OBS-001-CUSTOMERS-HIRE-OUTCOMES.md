# OBS-001 Customers Hire Outcomes

Status:
Draft

Version:
1.0

## Observation

Customers do not primarily buy AI models.

Customers buy outcomes.

Examples:

- More sales
- Better operations
- Lower cost
- Faster service
- Better decisions

## Implication

AI5R products should be framed around business outcomes, not only AI capabilities.
