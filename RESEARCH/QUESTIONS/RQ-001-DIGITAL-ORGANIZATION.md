Status:
Open

## Research Question

Can an organization itself become a deployable digital artifact?

## Background

Most AI systems focus on individual assistants, agents, or workflows.

AI5R investigates whether a full organizational structure can be represented, manufactured, deployed, operated, and governed digitally.

## Sub Questions

- What is the minimum structure required for a Digital Organization?
- Can departments, positions, capabilities, and policies be represented as reusable objects?
- How should Digital Organizations evolve over time?
- How should they be governed?
