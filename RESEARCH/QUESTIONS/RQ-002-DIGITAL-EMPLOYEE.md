## Research Question

What distinguishes a Digital Employee from a generic AI Agent?

## Background

AI agents are often defined by autonomy, tools, memory, and task execution.

AI5R investigates whether organizational identity, position, responsibility, governance, and accountability create a different category.

## Sub Questions

- Does a Digital Employee require organizational identity?
- Does a Digital Employee require a position?
- How does authority affect output?
- How should performance be evaluated?
