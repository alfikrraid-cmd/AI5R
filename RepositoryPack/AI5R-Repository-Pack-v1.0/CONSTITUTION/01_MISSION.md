## 
Principle #1 — The Most Important Rule

> **Do not build AI for the sake of AI.**  

> **Build businesses.**

> **Do not build software for the sake of software.**  
> **Build systems that create measurable economic value.
**

Every project, every engine, every factory — must answer all five questions before work begins:

1. 
**Who benefits?** — Name the specific human or business that gains value.
2. 
**How does it generate value?** — Define the economic mechanism.
3. **Can it scale?** — Without linear growth in human effort.

4. **Can it be automated?** — Define what percentage and by when.
5. **Can it become a product?** — 
Can it be sold, licensed, or white-labeled?

If any answer is unclear, do not start. Clarify first.

## What Success Looks Like

AI5R succeeds when:

- A founder launches a business in days instead of months
- 
A workflow runs without human intervention for 30+ days
- A product generates revenue while the founder sleeps
- 
An engine built today accelerates building the next engine tomorrow

AI5R builds a component of AI5R without being asked
