**Knowledge ID:** MR-KL-003
**Title:** Bundling modules of different verifiability in one order blurs confidence levels that should stay visible
**Source Manufacturing Order:** MO-001
**Source Manufacturing Review:** MR-001
**Evidence:** MO-001 reached a single "Release Candidate" determination covering both a genuinely-executed module (Basic AI Assistant) and five structurally-validated-only modules blocked on a missing database credential. The single label did not, by itself, communicate that these carried different strength of evidence — that distinction only became visible in the report's own per-module breakdown.
**Recommendation:** Even when one order-wide status is required, always accompany it with a per-module status breakdown so differing confidence levels remain visible rather than averaged away.
**Reuse Scope:** All future Manufacturing Orders and Manufacturing Reports.
