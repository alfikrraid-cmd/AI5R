**Knowledge ID:** MR-KQ-001
**Title:** Structural Validation and Runtime Verification must be two separate, mandatory determinations per module
**Source Manufacturing Order:** MO-001
**Source Manufacturing Review:** MR-001
**Evidence:** MO-001's Manufacturing Report separated these for each of its six new modules; the distinction was the only thing that made it possible to honestly state that five modules were structurally-validated-only while one (Basic AI Assistant) was genuinely executed. Without a mandatory, named gate for this, a future report could plausibly collapse the two into a single, overstated "PASS."
**Recommendation:** Every Quality Gate must include both determinations independently, per module, never one standing in for the other.
**Reuse Scope:** All future Manufacturing Orders. Reflected in `MANUFACTURING/TEMPLATES/QUALITY-GATE-TEMPLATE.md`.
